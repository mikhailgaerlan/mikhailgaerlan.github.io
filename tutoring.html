About Mike

Hello! My name is Mikhail "Mike" Gaerlan, and I am a recent MS Applied Mathematics graduate of the University of California, Davis. I also graduated summa cum laude from Mississippi State University in May 2016 with a Bachelor of Science in Physics and Mathematics and am an alumnus of the Mississippi School for Mathematics and Science.

I have a passion for learning and teaching and have been tutoring for over 10 years. I also have three years of teaching experience at the undergraduate level at UC Davis.

My mission is to provide you with high-quality personalized help in math and physics. I can tutor in a variety of test prep and undergraduate math and physics including ACT/SAT math, calculus, differential equations, and classical physics.
