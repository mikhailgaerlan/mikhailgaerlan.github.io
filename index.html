About Me

Hello! My name is Mikhail Gaerlan, and I am a graduate of the University of California, Davis with a Master of Science in Applied Mathematics. I also graduated summa cum laude from Mississippi State University in May 2016 with a Bachelor of Science in Physics and Mathematics and am an alumnus of the Mississippi School for Mathematics and Science.

I am currently a AI Engineer for Praxis Solutions applying my knowledge of LLMs, machine learning, mathematical modeling, data analysis, and optimization to wealth and asset management. I have over a decade of research and programming experience working on a variety of research and school projects which are summarized in my portfolio here.

I love programming and have used various languages including Python, Java, Fortran, R, and MATLAB among others for use in a variety of subjects such as network science, dark matter research, computational fluid dynamics, and accelerator, particle, and nuclear physics. For an overview of my research, check out my research page here.

Events

Outside of work, I'm a promoter for Switch WeHo, a monthly 21+ queer and Asian nightlife party at Beaches Tropicana in West Hollywood, produced by QT Nightlife. Having grown up in Mississippi, I never expected to find a community like this until I first attended one of their events. Now I help bring others into that same space in line with QT Nightlife's mission. So grab a ticket and I'll see you in WeHo!
