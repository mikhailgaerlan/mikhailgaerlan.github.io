Course Info

Introduction to integral calculus, elementary differential equations, and linear algebra via applications to biology and medicine. Fundamental theorem of calculus, techniques of integration including integral tables and numerical methods, improper integrals, elementary first order differential equations, linear systems, matrices, eigenvalues and eigenvectors, analytic geometry, applications in biology and medicine.
